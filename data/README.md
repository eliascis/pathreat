# Data inputs

This repository ships the directory layout the scripts expect, not the data. The raw inputs total roughly 100 GB and most of them are distributed under licences that do not permit redistribution. Intermediate pixel-level datasets (`data/store/*.fst`, about 100 GB) are regenerated by the pipeline. Three small derived files are tracked under `data/store/` because downstream scripts read them from there (see the bottom of this page).

Licence terms are summarised from the providers' sites at the time of download. Entries marked **verify** should be checked against the provider before reuse; nothing here replaces the provider's own terms.

## Pre-processed inputs (`data/store/pre-process/`)

| File | Content | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| `global_prematch_data.Rds` | 1-km pixel table: coordinates (Mollweide), matching covariates (elevation, population 2000, precipitation, slope, temperature, accessibility, crop suitability, tree cover 2000), biome, and the 14 threat indicators extracted from the rasters above | Compiled by the authors from the sources above; not redistributable because it embeds licensed layers at pixel level |
| `global_prematch_deforestation.Rds` | Yearly forest-loss pixel counts 2001-2020 | Derived from Hansen et al. 2013 Global Forest Change |

## Derived files tracked in this repository (`data/store/`)

| File | Content | Produced by |
|---|---|---|
| `Data_summary.xlsx` | Variable metadata: names, labels, units, sources, scale labels. Read by `code/pathreat.analysis.config.R` at startup | Authors |
| `pathreat.analysis.byPA.est.Rds` | One row per protected area (WDPA ID): composite-threat coefficient, SE, p-value, CI, adjusted levels, pair and observation counts, effectiveness class. No coordinates or PA attributes | `code/pathreat.analysis.byPA.est.R` |
| `pathreat.analysis.byPA-country-membership.Rds` | Protected area by country matched-pixel counts (transboundary attribution) | `code/pathreat.analysis.byPA.est.R` |
| `fig.*.csv` | Source data for the figures whose plotted values are computed live from pixel data (25-km grid means, country values, binned PA values, year counts) | the figure scripts, via `save_figure_data()` in the config |

## Intermediate datasets (not distributed)

`pathreat.data.merge.fst` (about 133 million pixels), `pathreat.data.merge.unmatched.fst` (about 127 million pixels, 157 countries), `pathreat.data.merge.matched.fst` (11.4 million observations in 5.7 million matched pairs, 107 countries, 22,090 protected areas), species rasters (one GeoTIFF per threatened species), and the matching stratum files. All are rebuilt by the data-preparation and matching scripts. The matched sample (1.6 GB) is the entry point for the estimation stage and can be requested from the corresponding author subject to the input providers' terms.
